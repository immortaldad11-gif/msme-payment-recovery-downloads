# MSME Payment Recovery — download

Get paid on time by buyers who pay late. The app tracks your bills from Tally, works out the
interest the MSMED Act allows, drafts polite-to-firm WhatsApp reminders for you to approve,
and prepares the filing pack for the MSME Samadhaan (ODR) portal.

## ⬇️ [Download for Windows](https://github.com/immortaldad11-gif/msme-payment-recovery-downloads/releases/latest/download/MSME-Payment-Recovery-Setup.exe)

This link always gives the newest version. Older versions are on the
[Releases](https://github.com/immortaldad11-gif/msme-payment-recovery-downloads/releases) page.

## Install

1. Download **MSME-Payment-Recovery-Setup.exe** (link above).
2. Double-click it. If Windows shows **"Windows protected your PC"**, click
   **More info → Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed yet).
3. Click **Install**, then **Finish**. The app opens in your browser; next time, use the desktop icon.
4. Choose a password, then enter your business details from the Udyam certificate.

No Python and no administrator rights are needed. To update, run the newer installer —
your data is kept.

**Tally:** in TallyPrime press **F1 → Settings → Connectivity → Client/Server configuration**,
set *TallyPrime acting as* **Server**, port **9000**, restart Tally and open your company.
Then click **Sync from Tally** in the app. The app only reads from Tally; it never changes your books.

## इंस्टॉल कैसे करें

1. ऊपर दिए लिंक से **MSME-Payment-Recovery-Setup.exe** डाउनलोड करें।
2. उस पर डबल-क्लिक करें। अगर **"Windows protected your PC"** दिखे, तो **More info → Run anyway** दबाएँ।
3. **Install** और फिर **Finish** दबाएँ। ऐप ब्राउज़र में खुल जाएगा; अगली बार डेस्कटॉप आइकन से खोलें।
4. पासवर्ड चुनें, फिर Udyam सर्टिफिकेट से अपने बिज़नेस की जानकारी भरें।

Python या एडमिन अधिकार की ज़रूरत नहीं है। नया वर्ज़न इंस्टॉल करने पर आपका डेटा सुरक्षित रहता है।
